---
description: Shift handover — what needs a decision today
---
Call the `morning_brief` MCP tool and turn the result into a handover a duty
officer can read in thirty seconds.

Structure it as:

1. One sentence: how many shipments need a decision and what idle fleet is costing.
2. The shipments needing a decision, worst clock first. For each: which clock is
   binding (schedule or stability), how many hours are left, and the money at risk.
3. Open excursions: severity, root cause, and who still has to sign.
4. Fleet: utilisation, what has been idle longest, and whether anything should be
   repositioned.

Do not propose approving anything yourself — name the decision and leave it.
